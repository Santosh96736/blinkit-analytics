# Blinkit Business Performance Analytics

## Project Overview
Developed an interactive analytics solution for Blinkit to monitor business performance across Sales, Customers, Delivery Operations, and Marketing Campaigns.

The project transforms operational data into actionable insights through four interconnected Tableau dashboards, enabling stakeholders to track key metrics, identify performance gaps, and support data-driven decision-making.

## Business Questions Answered
* How are revenue and orders trending over time?  
* Which products and categories generate the most revenue?  
* How effectively are customers being retained?  
* Are delivery operations meeting service expectations?  
* Which marketing channels generate the highest return on investment?  
* Where are customers dropping off in the marketing funnel?  

## Dashboards
### Sales Performance Dashboard

Tracks revenue trends, order behavior, top-performing products, category performance, and year-over-year growth.

![Sales Dashboard](https://github.com/Santosh96736/blinkit-analytics/blob/main/tableau/sales_dashboard.png)

### Customer Analytics Dashboard

Analyzes customer growth, repeat purchase behavior, order frequency, customer value, and revenue contribution.

### Delivery Performance Dashboard

Monitors delivery efficiency through on-time delivery rates, delay distribution, delivery time analysis, and delivery volume trends.

### Marketing Performance Dashboard

Evaluates campaign effectiveness using conversion rates, click-through rates, ROI analysis, marketing funnels, and channel performance.

## Key Insights
* Revenue showed a year-over-year decline, driven by weaker category performance.  
* Customer acquisition and engagement gradually decreased over time.  
* Most customers placed only one or two orders, highlighting retention opportunities.  
* Only 38% of deliveries were completed on time, indicating operational improvement potential.  
* Email campaigns generated the highest marketing ROI.  
* Significant drop-offs were observed between impressions and conversions, suggesting opportunities to improve campaign effectiveness.

## Business Recommendations

### Sales

* Focus promotional efforts on high-performing categories to maximize revenue growth.
* Investigate underperforming categories and optimize pricing, assortment, or promotional strategies.
* Schedule campaigns during peak revenue hours to improve sales efficiency.

### Customers

* Implement loyalty and retention programs to increase repeat purchases.
* Target one-time customers with personalized offers and product recommendations.
* Develop customer segmentation strategies to improve engagement and lifetime value.

### Delivery

* Analyze causes of delayed deliveries and optimize route planning and order allocation.
* Monitor delivery performance by region to identify operational bottlenecks.
* Establish delivery-time targets and track performance against service-level goals.

### Marketing

* Increase investment in high-ROI channels such as Email Marketing.
* Optimize marketing funnel stages to reduce drop-offs between impressions, clicks, and conversions.
* Continuously monitor campaign performance and reallocate budget toward the most effective channels.
